## Trademark policy

The goal is simple: people should know whether they are getting Dodaios from this
project or something else. In that spirit:

- **You may** use the name "Dodaios" to refer to the project truthfully: in articles,
  reviews, talks, tutorials, packages that ship our unmodified releases, and in phrases
  like "works with Dodaios" or "a plugin for Dodaios".
- **You may** fork the code and keep the name inside your fork for development, testing,
  and pull requests back to this project.
- **You must rename** a fork or redistribution that changes the software substantially,
  or that you ship as your own product or service (free or paid). Pick a name and logo
  that will not be confused with Dodaios, and do not present your version as the official
  one. A line such as "based on Dodaios" is welcome.
- **Do not** use the name or logo in a way that suggests this project endorses, sponsors,
  or is affiliated with you, your product, or your company, or in a domain name, app-store
  listing, or account name that could be mistaken for the project's own.

When in doubt, ask (see "Contact" below). We would rather say yes to a clear request than
find a confusing fork later.
