# Art direction notes (living log)

The art director's standing notes for every environment, prop and lighting
pass. Newest first. Read before every iteration.

## 2026-10-20 01:50 — no clipped reflections

Reflections of light panels in polished floors must never clip to flat white:
at the camera's angle a floor reflects a few per cent (Fresnel), so a
reflection lands at roughly 70–85 % value with soft streaks from the coat's
smudge breakup and a slight falloff at its edges. A flat white rectangle on the
floor reads as a floor light. The same holds for any highlight: gradient, never
a clipped slab.

## 2026-10-20 01:35 — first deck previews

- **Emblems (legal):** no round emblem with spokes, cogs or hexagons in a
  circle — those evoke a film faction's crest. The ship's mark is a plain
  wedge/chevron or the deck number in a simple frame.
- **Text on floors and walls:** at most one bay/deck number per room plus small
  worn stencils. Large painted words read as game UI.
- **No warm wood or beige** anywhere; tables are steel, composite or black
  gloss. Hull filler is dark gunmetal, never brown.
- **Launch well / shafts:** deep and dark with visible inner structure and
  small descending light rings; a field is a faint translucent sheen, not a
  coloured slab.
- **Render artefacts:** smeared streaks, blotches or fireflies are bugs; raise
  samples or remove the cause.

## 2026-10-19 23:30 — contrast is the signature

From the reference review (private contact sheet) and the first lookdev:

1. **Mostly dark, crisply lit.** Rooms are lit by rows of bright ceiling
   panels (camera-invisible, but mirrored in the floor) and by wall light
   columns; between them the space falls to near black. Avoid large flat
   mid-grey areas. Brightest values: light panels and their reflections.
2. **Hero wall motif.** Dark gunmetal/charcoal wall panelling with *vertical
   columns of stacked, backlit white rectangles* in recessed bays, alternating
   with dark grilles and ribs. Light-grey panelling is the secondary, cleaner
   variant for offices, briefing and med rooms.
3. **Black mirror floors.** Very low roughness with a subtle large-scale
   smudge/scuff breakup; reflections are crisp rectangles cut by the 1.2 m
   plate seams, never blurry blobs. Grated floors in hangar/engineering.
4. **Accents are small.** Red and white indicator lights, a thin red trim on
   console tops, amber only for warnings. Hazard stripes only as thin, worn
   bands on step and pit edges — never whole striped pillars.
5. **No haze in ordinary rooms.** Haze only in the reactor shaft and the
   hangar, uniform and low, never blotchy.
6. **No voids.** Every pixel of a plate is ship: structure, conduits,
   neighbouring rooms, dim maintenance light.
7. **Consoles** are grey/black with muted key banks, a few small lit
   indicators, sloped screen bays, steel edge trim; screens dark blue-green
   with thin readouts.
8. **Scale.** Huge rooms, small people: generous floor area, tall far walls,
   rhythm of 1.2 m modules, symmetry around the main axis.
